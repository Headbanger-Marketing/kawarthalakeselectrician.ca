The charger is only one part of the installation

An EV charging project includes equipment, a suitable connection and an assessment of the property supply. ESA advises using approved charging equipment, a dedicated circuit and outdoor-rated equipment where needed. The contractor should evaluate the proposed load and explain whether the supply, a load-management arrangement or another design is suitable. Do not choose a circuit from the charger advertising alone.

Prepare the scope for your Kawartha Lakes property

Sketch the path from the house to the parking spot in words, noting any driveway, lawn or yard crossing along the way. Record whether the garage is heated, has a finished ceiling and what else already runs there.

Ask who will dig or open walls along the route, whether outdoor-rated equipment is planned for that spot and how the proposal keeps the charger apart from shop equipment already in that building.

What should the assessment establish?

A charging position away from the house, at a detached garage or a distant parking spot in Kawartha Lakes, sits at the end of a route from the supply, and the contractor traces all of it. Any feed already reaching the outbuilding matters too: the contractor notes whether one exists and whether it was weighed alongside the charger. Any shop or hobby equipment counted in the load assessment is named, and the contractor explains that assessment in plain terms. The scope lists the approved equipment, the dedicated circuit, weather protection at the spot and any ground or wall work the route requires.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance, and EV charging safety guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Can a charger run from a detached garage that has its own feed?

Possibly, though the contractor has to look at the feed already reaching that building, its condition and its existing users. Get that review in writing before you pick or purchase a unit.

Will a parking spot far from the house complicate charger wiring?

Distance can change the cable route, the equipment that suits the spot and the work needed between the two points. Have the contractor walk through the route and describe what it crosses.
